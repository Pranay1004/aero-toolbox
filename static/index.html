What is the reentry corridor?
The corridor is the altitude band at each velocity where the vehicle can safely fly.
Upper boundary = equilibrium glide (lift balances gravity, Eq. 15.47)
Lower boundary = min of: heat flux limit (15.48), load factor limit (15.49), dynamic pressure limit (15.50)
Status OK = corridor open (h_upper > h_lower). NO = corridor closed.
